Stealth Mode
Enable Stealth Mode
Stay completely invisible and undetectable while using SilentGPT
How to use Stealth Mode:
1 Enable Stealth Mode and press Save Settings
2 Open SilentGPT and activate the overlay (Alt+1 or your hotkey)
3 Then open the app you want to use SilentGPT with
4 Use stealth hotkeys to control SilentGPT (see below)
5 When done, press Escape to hide the overlay
